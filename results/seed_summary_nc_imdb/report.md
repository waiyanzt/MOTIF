# IMDb node classification: seed-averaged results

MOTIF classifies by link prediction to one node per class; only training nodes are linked to their class, so it sees training labels as graph edges and no node features. Baselines (RGCN, SlotGAT: lab original arm) use plot-keyword features. Mean ± population std over training seeds (20241017, 20251017, 1566911444); zero-shot has no training seed (std 0).
